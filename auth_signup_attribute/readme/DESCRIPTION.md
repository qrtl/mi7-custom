This module does the following:

- Asks the visitor on the signup page whether the account is for an individual
  or a company, and stores the answer in the partner's customer type.
- Adds a date of birth field to the signup page. It is required for an
  individual, and hidden for a company as a company does not have one.

The name field is kept as a single field, and holds the company name when the
company type is selected.
